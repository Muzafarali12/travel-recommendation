About Us

TravelBloom is a travel recommendation company founded in 2026. We collect popular beaches, temples and cities from around the world so travellers can plan trips with confidence. Every destination on our site comes with photos and a short, honest description.

Our Mission

To inspire people to explore the world by offering clear, friendly and visual travel recommendations.

Guidelines for Team Members

  1. Check that every recommendation has an accurate name, a clear photo and a short description.
  2. Use only images you own or have permission to use.
  3. Keep the tone friendly and simple, and avoid exaggerated claims.
  4. Test search keywords (beach, temple, country) after every update.
  5. Reply to messages from the Contact Us page within two working days.
  6. Commit changes to GitHub with a clear message describing what changed.
